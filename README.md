## Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce retail stall at Gikomba Market, manually tracking daily revenue and inventory levels. To sustain and optimize her operations, she needs quantitative insight into total revenue health, trajectory across weeks, customer buying patterns between weekdays and weekends, critical inventory restocking triggers, and individual product gross margins using core Python data structures.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Massive Weekend Sales Premium:** Weekend sales average **KES 4,900.00** per day, outperforming the weekday average of **KES 2,685.00** by **+82.50%**.
2. **Positive Week-on-Week Trajectory:** Overall revenue rose from **KES 22,050.00** in Week 1 to **KES 24,400.00** in Week 2, delivering a solid **+10.66%** growth rate.
3. **Critical Stockout Risk on Core High-Margin Essentials:** Both **Tomatoes** (18 kg in stock vs. 25 kg reorder threshold) and **Sukuma** (9 kg in stock vs. 15 kg threshold) have dropped into deficit, threatening revenue on items yielding **33.33%** and **40.00%** gross profit margins.
